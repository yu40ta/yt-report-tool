Privacy Policy

YT Report Tool is a personal script used only by its developer for the developer's own YouTube channel.

Data accessed

  • YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, subscribers, traffic sources, countries) of the authorizing user's own channel, through the read-only scopes yt-analytics.readonly and youtube.readonly.
  • Basic channel and video information (titles, privacy status, upload dates) of the same channel.

Use and storage

  • The data is used only to produce weekly and monthly summaries for the channel owner.
  • The data and the authorization token are stored only on the developer's own computer.
  • The data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the "Third-party apps & services" page of the Google Account.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
